What songs are confirmed in GTA 6?

CONFIRMED

Six, all heard in the two official trailers: Tom Petty's 'Love Is a Long Road' (Trailer 1), then 'Hot Together' by The Pointer Sisters, 'Thunder Island' by Jay Ferguson, 'Everybody Have Fun Tonight' by Wang Chung, 'Talkin' to Myself Again' by Tammy Wynette and 'Child Support' by Zenglen (Trailer 2).

How we call it

  • CONFIRMED: Rockstar or Take-Two said it or showed it in official material: the Newswire, the official site, the official trailers, or official store copy. Or a major outlet reported that official statement.
  • RUMOR: a real claim is going around (leaks, insiders, analysts), but Rockstar has not confirmed it. Leaked footage is never confirmation here, no matter how real it looks.
  • NOT CONFIRMED: people ask, but there is no official answer at all. We say so instead of guessing.
